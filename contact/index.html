FAQ

Questions we often get

Can’t find your answer? Call (408) 255-4800.

Where is Viji’s Beauty Salon?

We’re at 10281 S De Anza Blvd, Cupertino, CA 95014, in the Allario Center shopping plaza on South De Anza Boulevard near Rodrigues Avenue. It’s an easy drive from Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, San Jose and Los Altos.

What are your hours?

We’re open every day of the week, 10 AM to 8 PM.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. To get the time and stylist you want, especially on weekends, send a booking request online, on WhatsApp or call us.

How much is eyebrow threading?

Eyebrow threading is $10 ($12 for men). Upper lip is $5 and full face threading starts at $35. See every price on our services page.

Do you do bridal makeup and mehandi?

Yes. Bridal makeup and hair starts at $250 and bridal mehandi starts at $175. Contact us early with your wedding date so we can hold it for you.

Does threading hurt?

Most people feel a quick tingle rather than pain, and it gets easier with regular visits. Our threaders work fast and in small sections to keep it comfortable.

Is there parking?

Yes. The shopping plaza has a parking lot right outside the salon.

Why do some prices say “& up”?

Those services depend on hair length, thickness or the area covered. We’ll always tell you the exact price before we start.
