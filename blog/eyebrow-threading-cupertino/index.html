Eyebrow threading in Cupertino: what to expect at your first visit

New to eyebrow threading? Here’s what happens in the chair, how it feels, how long it lasts and how to care for your brows. Threading in Cupertino from $10.

Threading is one of the oldest hair-removal methods there is, and still one of the most precise. If you’ve only ever had your brows waxed or tweezed, here’s what to expect when you try eyebrow threading in Cupertino for the first time.

What is threading?

Your threader twists a thin cotton thread into a loop and rolls it across the skin. As the twist moves, it catches rows of tiny hairs and lifts them out from the root. Because the thread only touches the hair, not the skin around it, threading is a good choice if wax leaves you red, or if you use retinol or other active skin care.

What happens in the chair

  1. A quick chat. Tell us the shape you like, or show a photo. If you’re growing your brows out, say so and we’ll only tidy the strays.
  2. Shaping. You’ll usually be asked to hold your skin taut near the brow. Your threader works in small sections, checking the symmetry as they go.
  3. Finishing. A final check in the mirror, a trim if your brow hairs are long, and something soothing for the skin.

The whole thing takes just a few minutes, which is why so many people fit it into a lunch break.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe a quick tingle or a pinch rather than pain, and it gets easier with regular visits because the hair grows back finer. Your skin may look a little pink for half an hour afterwards. That’s normal and settles quickly.

How long does it last?

Most clients come back every two to four weeks. Hair grows in cycles, so regular threading keeps your shape crisp and makes each appointment quicker.

Aftercare tips

  • Skip makeup, exfoliants and heavy creams on the area for a few hours.
  • Avoid saunas, steam rooms and strong sun for the rest of the day.
  • Don’t pluck in between visits. Let the shape grow evenly so we can keep it balanced.

More than brows

Threading works just as well for the upper lip, chin, forehead and sideburns, or the whole face at once. See all our threading prices: eyebrows are $10, upper lip $5 and full face from $35.
