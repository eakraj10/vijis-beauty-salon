Bridal makeup tips: how to plan your wedding-day look

A simple bridal beauty timeline: when to book your makeup artist, skin prep, mehandi timing and what to bring on the day. Bridal makeup in Cupertino from $250.

Your wedding photos will be around for decades, so it’s worth planning your bridal look as carefully as the venue. Here’s a simple timeline to follow if you’re booking bridal makeup in Cupertino.

3–6 months before: book your artist

Wedding season dates go quickly. Book your bridal makeup and hair as soon as you have a date, and ask about mehandi at the same time so everything is in one place.

4–8 weeks before: start your skin routine

  • Keep it simple and consistent: gentle cleanser, moisturiser and daily sunscreen.
  • Book a facial four to six weeks out, not the week of the wedding. That gives your skin time to settle. A guide to picking the right facial can help.
  • Don’t try new strong products (peels, retinoids, new acne treatments) close to the day.

2–4 weeks before: trial and colour decisions

A makeup trial is the best way to agree on a look before the day. Bring photos of looks you love, a picture of your outfit and jewellery, and tell us how you want to feel: soft and natural, or full glam. If you’re colouring your hair, do it about two weeks out so the colour settles.

1 week before: threading and waxing

Have your eyebrows threaded and any waxing done five to seven days before. Any redness will be long gone, and your brows will be at their neatest.

1–2 days before: mehandi

Henna darkens over the first 24 to 48 hours, so applying bridal mehandi a day or two before the wedding gives the deepest colour on the day. Keep it on as long as you can and avoid washing the area for the first few hours after removal.

On the day

  • Arrive with clean, dry hair (unless we’ve agreed otherwise) and a bare, moisturised face.
  • Wear a top that buttons or zips at the front, so you don’t pull anything over your hair.
  • Bring your lipstick for touch-ups, and eat something before we start.
