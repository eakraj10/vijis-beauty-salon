How to choose a facial for your skin type

Dry, oily, acne-prone or dull skin? A plain-English guide to choosing the right facial, how often to go and what to avoid after. Facials in Cupertino from $45.

“Which facial should I get?” is a question we hear a lot at our Cupertino salon. The honest answer depends on your skin and what you want from the visit. Here’s a quick guide.

Start with your skin type

Dry or tight skin

Look for a facial focused on hydration and massage. An oxygen facial or a full facial with a longer massage helps skin feel softer and look plumper. Follow up with a richer moisturiser at home.

Oily or acne-prone skin

Choose deep cleansing over heavy creams. Our acne treatment focuses on clearing congestion. Avoid picking at spots before your appointment, and tell us about any prescription treatments you use.

Dull or tired skin

If your skin looks flat rather than troubled, a brightening facial works well. The fresh fruit, pearl and gold facials are popular before events and photos, and the diamond facial is our premium option for weddings and big occasions.

Sensitive skin

Go gentle. A mini facial or the botanical Biotique facial is a good first step. Always mention allergies, and skip strong exfoliation before your visit.

How often should you get a facial?

Every four to six weeks suits most people, roughly one skin cycle. Before a big event, book your facial a week or two ahead, not the day before, so any redness has time to settle.

After your facial

  • Keep makeup off for the rest of the day if you can.
  • Skip saunas, intense workouts and strong sun for 24 hours.
  • Hold off on exfoliants and retinoids for a couple of days.
